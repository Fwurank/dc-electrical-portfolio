Projects

Four connected studies of the same campus. Each project feeds its ratings into the next, and the design basis is updated to Rev P2 with all of them.

PROJECT 01

Design basis & single-line diagrams

Design basis DB-001 Rev P2 for a 30 MW IT campus: two 130 kV lines, 2 × 63 MVA 130/20 kV transformers, six 5 MW halls with 24 power blocks of 2.5 MVA in distributed-redundant 4N/3. Eight A3 single-line diagrams drawn in Python with IEC 60617 symbols; every text is measured at ≥ 2.5 mm on A3 by an automated checker.

PROJECT 02

Load & equipment sizing tool

Python package and Streamlit app that turn IT load into facility load and size transformers, gensets, UPS/batteries, cables and busway in normal and failure modes. Design peak 37.8 MW, but 42.6 MW with UPS recharge, which exceeds the 40 MW subscription. Annualised PUE 1.165. The battery per UPS module goes from 120 to 165 kWh because of the C-rate limit.

PROJECT 03

Short-circuit, protection & arc-flash study

Own IEC 60909 nodal solver over 7 scenarios, cross-checked with pandapower: MSB 52.3 kA / ip 122 kA. Closing the generator back onto the grid would give 81.8 kA against 65 kA switchgear, so retransfer is open-transition only. Paralleling T1 and T2 would load the 20 kV peak rating to 94 %, so that interlock stays. Optical arc protection with MV intertrip cuts the LV arc-flash energy from 96 to 3.2 cal/cm².

PROJECT 04

BESS in a grid-constrained data center

10 MW / 20 MWh BESS behind a 40 MW grid subscription, using LP dispatch. It turns −2.08 MW of headroom into +2.41 MW of extra IT capacity, of which only +0.84 MW is firm at N-1. It gives 27 min of backup but cannot replace the gensets: a 381 A island fault is not cleared by the 20 kV relays. Revenue stacking brings ≈ 1.82 M€/yr (2026 prices), NPV ≈ 7.2 M€. AI-training swings need about 10 ms response.

  • FCR / mFRR

Assumptions & limitations

What the results depend on, and what this work is not.

Key assumptions

  • Grid strength at 130 kV: 3 000 / 1 500 MVA, X/R 10. All fault levels scale with it, and the T1//T2 margin is lost above ≈ 4 000 MVA.
  • Climate: annual mean +4.5 °C, design +32 °C. PUE and the design peak depend on these.
  • Vendor-typical data: UPS efficiency curve, LFP end of life 0.8 and C-rate ≤ 8 C, genset x′d 0.22, busway Icw 36 kA, PCS fault current 1.2 × In.
  • BESS economics: Svk / Mimer capacity prices for 2025 and 2026 plus a "saturated" case, realisation 0.85, CAPEX 120 €/kWh + 75 €/kW × 1.2.

Limitations

  • Concept-level calculations, not validated in a commercial tool (ETAP, PowerFactory). Project 03 is cross-checked with pandapower.
  • Generic LV trip curves. Selectivity and cascade tables need the selected manufacturer's data.
  • The 20 kV arc flash uses the conservative Lee method. Earthing is screened only; a soil model and DSO data are still needed.
  • BESS profiles are synthetic, hourly and assume perfect foresight. Market depth is not modelled, and Tier III / EN 50600 statements are interpretations, not certification advice.
