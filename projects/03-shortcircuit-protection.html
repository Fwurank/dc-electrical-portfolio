03 · Short-circuit, protection & arc-flash study

scprot: an IEC 60909 short-circuit solver written from scratch, protection coordination from 20 kV down to the rack PDU, CT checks, arc-flash energy and a comparison of 20 kV neutral earthing options.

Tool cross-check. The same study cases have also been run in DIgSILENT PowerFactory, using its short-circuit, protection and arc-flash modules. Those PowerFactory results are still being prepared and will be uploaded to this repository later. Pandapower is used here so that the results can be reproduced and cross-checked with open-source code.

Key findings

  • Seven scenarios were solved with a nodal-admittance method and cross-checked with pandapower (identical in the max case). Results: 20 kV 13.0 kA; MSB-A 52.3 kA / ip 122 kA.
  • T1//T2 paralleling gives ip 58.5 kA, which is 94 % of the 62.5 kA rating and over 100 % at S″k 4 000 MVA, so the interlock stays. Closed-transition retransfer gives 81.8 kA > 65 kA, so retransfer is open-transition only.
  • The busway start sees 37.1 kA against an Icw of 36 kA, so the feeder needs a current-limiting MCCB (Icu ≥ 50 kA, tested conditional ≥ 40 kA). The rack inlet sees 14.0 kA, so the PDU needs an SCCR of ≥ 15 kA.
  • Arc flash (IEEE 1584-2018): optical arc relays with MV intertrip reduce the MSB line side from 96 to 3.2 cal/cm². At 20 kV (Lee method) it goes from ≈ 106 to 11 cal/cm². Compensated earthing is kept: Ic is 22 A per section.
