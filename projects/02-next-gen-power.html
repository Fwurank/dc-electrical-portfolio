02 · Next-generation power architecture and commissioning (research and concept study)

An 800 VDC architecture comparison with a concept single-line diagram for power block A, and a commissioning and test plan concept, both for the fictional 30 MW IT campus of Projects 01 and 03–05. Version v0.1, 2026-10-29.

Research and concept study, not built work. Project 02 is a research and concept study on the same fictional 30 MW campus as Projects 01 and 03–05. Nothing in it has been built, tested or commissioned. Both documents are marked CONCEPT / DRAFT — requires review by a licensed electrical engineer, and statements carry credibility labels: [Verified], [Vendor claim], [Search-level], [Unverified] or [Inference].

Key findings

  • Part 1 compares 400 V AC + UPS, 400 VDC and 800 VDC for one 5 MW hall over 13 rows, and redraws block A as an 800 VDC concept (SLD-005). The main open risk for 800 VDC is DC bus protection and the arc-flash method [Inference].
  • Part 2 is a concept L0–L5 commissioning and test plan for hall DH1 with 11 integrated-systems-test scenarios, genset and MV switchgear selection points, and what would change in Finland.
  • All ratings come unchanged from DB-001 Rev P2 and CALC-003 / CALC-004. No rating of Projects 01 and 03–05 is changed by this study.
This is a research and concept study on a fictional case, built from public standards, public sources and assumptions. It is not a design for construction and describes no work done or commissioned. No employer, client or vendor data is used.

Part 1 · 800 VDC architecture comparison

DWU-DC-E-NOTE-005 v0.1 (2026-10-29), A3 one-pager. CONCEPT / DRAFT — requires review by a licensed electrical engineer.

  • Three options for one 5 MW hall (DH1, blocks A–D) with the same 4N/3 redundancy as DB-001 Rev P2: A · 400 V AC + static UPS (the Project 01 baseline), B · 400 VDC class (260–400 V), C · 800 VDC (±400 V). 13 rows: topology, conversion stages, feeder current, fault current, protection, earthing, arc flash, storage, grid interface, gensets, maturity, risks and missing data.
  • Pair-group feeder current at 0.83 MW in N-1: ≈ 1 200 A per phase (AC), ≈ 2 180 A at 380 V DC, ≈ 1 040 A at 800 V DC [Inference, I = P / U]. "45 % less copper" is a [Vendor claim] and is not used as a design input.
  • Open points for 800 VDC: SSCB vs thermal-magnetic protection is still open [Verified, Dell'Oro (KB)]; NFPA 70E Annex D.5 DC arc-flash methods are informative only [Search-level]; the IEEE P3002.12 DC arcing-fault guide is an active PAR, approved 2026-09-25 [Verified]. OCP Mt. Diablo v1.0.0 is effective from 2026-10-08 [Verified (KB)]; the OCP SST spec v0.3.0 is an early draft [Search-level].
  • Conclusion [Inference]: option C with an LF transformer (SLD-005 main scheme) keeps the MV design, the gensets and IL-3 of Project 01 unchanged and moves the risk into one place, the 800 V DC bus protection and arc-flash method. The SST variant also changes the genset and MV protection concept, so it is not taken into a Tier III / EN 50600 Class 3 design until the OCP SST spec is past v0.x. Option B gives no current advantage over AC at these rack sizes.

Part 2 · Commissioning & test plan (concept)

DWU-DC-E-CX-005 v0.1 (2026-10-29), A4, 4 pages. CONCEPT / DRAFT — requires review by a licensed electrical engineer. It describes no work done or signed for.

  • Commissioning levels L0–L5 (design review, FAT, delivery and installation, start-up, functional, integrated systems test) applied to hall DH1. The level names are industry practice, not one standard [Search-level]; the campus content and acceptance values are proposals [Inference].
  • A constraint found in the Project 01 design: the Q-LB load-bank connection, 2 500 A at 400 V, carries ≈ 1.73 MVA. The block N-1 design load of 1.67 MW can be proven through it, but the IT genset's full 2 200 kWe / 2 750 kVA cannot [Inference, arithmetic]. Options are raised for the L0 design review.
  • 11 integrated-systems-test scenarios, from the loss of one 130 kV line to UPS battery discharge, MBB transfer, arc-flash trip and retransfer (open transition only, because closed transition would give 81.8 kA against 65 kA).
  • Genset and MV switchgear selection points: DCC rating basis, sustained Isc ≥ 300 % for 2 s, HVO100 for 24 h; EU F-gas Regulation 2024/573 Art. 13(9)(a) prohibits putting F-gas MV switchgear ≤ 24 kV into operation from 1 January 2026 [Verified, EC FAQ].
  • What would change in Finland: the Fingrid KJV2026 draft for demand facilities above 30 MW, including data centres [Verified], and the SFS 6000 / 6001 installation series [Search-level]. Ten questions to ask on day one.
